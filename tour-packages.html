Kaziranga Tour Packages

Spread over an area of 429 square kilometers, Kaziranga National Park is a wildlife ecotourism hotspot covering the Golaghat and Nagaon districts in the heart of Assam. KNP is one of the most celebrated wildlife destinations in India. Its unique biological sphere and availability of varied flora and fauna makes it most visited tourism destination in India. This protected area was discovered in the year of 1904 by the wife of Lord Curzon, who then developed it into a Reserve forest within the next four years. And in 1950 it was christened as Kaziranga wildlife sanctuary. Subsequently, in 1985 UNESCO declared Kaziranga as a World Heritage Site for wildlife. Since then it attracts thousands of tourists every year. Apart from one-horned rhino, it protects more than 491 avifaunal species that’s why Birdlife International has designated Kaziranga as an important bird area.
