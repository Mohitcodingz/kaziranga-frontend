North East Golden Triangle Tour

Packages

North East Golden Triangle Tour

Duration

06 Night -07 Days

Location

Assam, India

Description

North East Golden Triangle Tour is the most demanding tour package when you wish you explore maximum part of the Assam. Guwahati the capital of the state is bordered with mountains, greenery and mighty Brahamputra River is one of the beautiful city of Assam. Kaziranga is one of popular wildlife destination of the country. It is UNESCO declared world heritage site. The third destination Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya has been also bestowed with all the natural bounties. An itinerary through these beautiful destinations would be truly unforgettable in life.

Tour Duration

  • Days 01

    Guwahati - Kaziranga National Park (250 Kms/ 05 Hrs.)

    Once you reach at Guwahati airport our tour representative who will assist you to board the taxi and transfer to Kaziranga National Park via road. Kaziranga - a great territory of one-horned Rhinos (203 Kms / 05 hrs). After reaching, check into your hotel/resort. Freshen up yourself and take refreshment. Spend rest of the time in leisure as you wish. In evening you may enjoy the local traditional cultural programs performed by the tribal. Have dinner on time and retire for the night.

  • Days 02

    Explore Kaziranga National Park

    Wake up early morning with a hot cup of tea/coffee and get ready for the jungle safari. You head towards the Eastern Zone of the Kaziranga National Park on back of big mammal Elephant in search of wildlife. Now spend beautiful moment with your loved ones listening to the music of the jungle and wildlife viewing and nature walk in the park. Apart from one-horned Rhinos, other exotic species found are Swamp Deer, Water buffalo, Asiatic Elephants and moreover little bit luck is needed to spot the tigers in territory of Kaziranga. Later come back to resort, enjoy lunch and dinner, overnight stay in a wildlife resort.

  • Days 03

    Kaziranga National Park - Shillong (260 Kms / 06 Hrs)

    After having breakfast at the resort complete check out formalites from the resort on time and transfer to Shillong by road in a comfortable vehicle. Six hours of drive takes you through a majestic placid lake with it surrounding sylvan hills. Upon arrival at Shillong check into the hotel, take refreshment and spend rest of the time at leisure. In evening you may enjoy the beauty of local street markets, later come back to hotel and take dinner and retire for the night.

  • Days 04

    Sightseeing In Mokokchung

    The fourth day of your tour we take you on a full day excursion. Shillong, popularly known as "Scottland of the East" is bestowed with splendid waterfalls cascading down deep valleys forming misty sparks as they break the silence of the surroundings. Afternoon is left leisure for shopping. You may opt for Cherapunjee - the wettest place in the world (65 Kms/ 02 hrs). At last come back to the hotel enjoy dinner and overnight stay in a hotel.

  • Days 05

    Sightseeing In Shillong

    After enjoying breakfast check out from the hotel on time and transfer to Guwahati by surface in a comfortable vehicle. Enroute visit Cathedral Church, Ward's Lake, and Lady Hydrae Park. Arrive Guwahati and check into the hotel. Take lunch and after a short break get ready to visit 'Kalakshetra', a place where Assamese history and culture presents through Light and sound show. Return to hotel. Dinner and stay at hotel.

  • Days 06

    Explore Beautiful Guwahati

    After having breakfast at the hotel get ready to explore the rustic beauty of Guwahati. Visit the most sacred Hindu places like Kamakhya Temple, Tirupati Balaji Temple and Navagraha Temple. Do not forget to ask your guide to take you to the Assam State Museum. After a lunch break, again we start to visit State Zoo cum Botanical Garden, Regional Science Centre and Sukreswar Temple (The 6th Jyotirlinga of Shiva). Evening we suggest an optional tour of Golden sunset cruise on the Brahmaputra. Stay overnight in Guwahati.

  • Days 07

    Departure Onward Destination

    After having breakfast complete the check out formalities at the hotel and leave for Guwahati airport to catch the flight. Return home with bunch of sweet and loving memories.
