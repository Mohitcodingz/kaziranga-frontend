Kaziranga Weekend Tour

Duration

2 Night -03 Days

Location

Assam, India

Description

Visiting any Wildlife Sanctuary in India gives an experience of apex form of tourism. And Kaziranga National Park in Assam is one such wildlife spot known for its abundance green vegetation, various wildlife species, picturesque valleys and pleasant weather throughout the year. This nice holiday place is great for all those travelers who wish to enjoy panoramic natural beauty and wild thrills. Passing through the jungles of Kaziranga which is bestowed with tall elephant grasses, makes your weekend tour unforgettable. We have tailored this itinerary under the supervision of highly expert tour guide which includes Jeep and Elephant Safari ride.

Travel Days

  • Days 01

    Guwahati – Kaziranga National Park (217 kilometers 4.5 hours drive)

    You have to reach up to Guwahati airport. Upon arrival at airport you will be escorted by our tour executive and further drive to Kaziranga National Park via surface in a comfortable vehicle. After you reach Kaziranga complete the check-in formalities at the pre-booked hotel and freshen up yourself. Enjoy the rest of the time at leisure and in evening you may go for local village sightseeing. Overnight stay in the hotel.

  • Days 02

    Explore Kaziranga National Park

    This morning is dedicated to explore the flora and fauna of Kaziranga Natinal Park. Get up early in the morning with hot cup of tea. Be prepared for the morning Jeep/Elephant safari as per your booking requested. We will pick up from your resort and take you to the jungle of Kaziranga for thrilling jeep safari. Explore the untouched beauty of Kaziranga. Late in the afternoon after taking lunch you may go for a tribal village visit to see silk worm rearing by Assamese. After enjoying the rustic beauty come back to the resort, take dinner and overnight stay in a wildlife resort.

  • Days 03

    Kaziranga National Park – Guwahati (217 kilometers 4.5 hours drive)

    Begin your day with hot cup of tea or coffee. Take your breakfast in resort and complete the checkout formalities. Drive to Guwahati via road to catch your flight from Guwahati for your onward destination. Go back home with sweet and loving memories.
