Online Safari Booking Kaziranga National Park

Thank you for visiting our website choosing us for booking Kaziranga Online Safari. We provide the most convenient and hassle free online safari booking platform. Our safari booking procedure is very simple and fast. Just fill up with your details and select type of safari with your suitable time slot and we do rest for you.

We offer facility of online safari booking for Jeep Safari and Elephant Safari as well. The booking for Jeep safari ride can be reserved for all the four tourism zones of the Kaziranga Tiger Reserve – Bagori Zone, Kohora, Agaratoli, and Burapahar Zone; whereas the booking for Elephant ride can be made for two zones namely Kohora and Bagori. Please note that safari booking is monitored and administered by the Kaziranga forest officials. Our easy and smooth online platform is fully abided by the rules and regulations of the Kaziranga forest officials. You can enjoy our facility by making the payment of full safari amount in advance which includes entry permit fee, nature guide fee and safari vehicle fee.
In jeep safari 6 travelers can go at a time in the park while elephant can carry maximum 4 people. The entry permit for jeep safari is charged per jeep while entry for elephant ride is charged per person. All the four ecotourism zones of the Kaziranga National Park is dotted with unique landscape and are enriched with various variety of flora and fauna. There are no such things like good zone or bad zone, every zone has its unique trait and best for wildlife sightings. The majorly found wild animals in the Kaziranga include Rhinoceros, Indian Elephant, Tiger, Water Buffalo and Swamp Deer along with other wildlife are equally found in each zone and you will get a good sighting of wildlife during your safari.

Kaziranga Online Safari Booking Procedure :-
For fast and smooth booking process please help us with following details to go ahead.
  • The full name, age, gender of every travelers same as printed on your identity cards
  • Please provide us with your preferred date of travel and safari timing (morning/evening)
  • Send copy of your original ID cards (voter ID, Aadahar No., Driving License No. etc)
  • Safari Booking amount as conveyed should be paid full in advance
  • Please note that E-permits charges are non-refundable once issued
  • Foreign travelers required to send their full passport details
  • Safari seats are issued on first come and first serve basis (subject to availability)
  • Please carry your ID in original same produced during online booking
Things to Note before Booking Kaziranga Safari Online
  • Please do not make half payment; in such case safari permit is not generated
  • We cannot change the booking process, it is managed by forest officials KNP
  • Entry permit issued in your name cannot be transferred or cancelled
  • Only registered vehicles and elephants are allowed to enter inside the park
  • Safari is conducted on designated route of the officials, please do not ask to change
  • During peak season there is a scarcity of the seats so book well in advance
  • Your entry permits or park may be closed in any unavoidable circumstances without any prior notice. We cannot change it.
  • In case of any changes in entry fees or permit charges, travelers are liable to pay the difference amount at the time of making entry inside the park
  • The visitors are required to report the starting point of the safari 15 minute prior to the departure of the safari
  • Please confirm your pick and drop facility from your accommodation sight. We offer on special request and on making extra payment for it
