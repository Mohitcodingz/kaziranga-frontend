Kaziranga Rhino Tour – Wildlife Safari

Duration

06 Night -07 Days

Location

Assam, India

Description

This special tour package has been designed for sightings of world fame Indian One-Horned Rhino. Our itinerary also takes you to the most part of the north east which is blessed with bounty of natural beauty and some of the best spot of Assam. Kaziranga Tour Package provides you the rare sight of the exotic one-horned-rhinoceros and on the other hand Shillong welcomes you with picture perfect scenery. If you wish to enjoy a tour to both destinations then avail this wildlife tour package.

Destination Covered – Guwahati – Kaziranga National Park – Shillong – Guwahati

  • Days 01

    Arrival at Guwahati Airport

    Once you reach Guwahati Airport you will be accompanied by one of our tour executive and drive to city. Complete the check-in formalities at the hotel which is pre-booked for you. Freshen up yourself and take your meal. Rest of the time in leisure, evening is spared to explore the city Guwahati. Overnight stay.

  • Days 02

    Guwahati – Kaziranga National Park

    Second day is going to be bit busy. Take your breakfast and on time and get ready to visit Kamakhya Temple – a major Hindu religious site. It is very historical temple. Come back to hotel after paying homage to the mother goddess Kamakhya and get ready for drive on to famous Kaziranga National park (220 kms 4 hrs of drive). Check into a pre-booked Jungle resort on arrival. In the afternoon you may visit nearby Tea Estate. Stay overnight at Kaziranga.

  • Days 03

    Explore Kaziranga National Park

    Get up early in the morning today and freshen up yourself. Take tea at 5:30 and be prepared to head towards central range of Kaziranga for elephant safari ride. The varied flora and fauna of Kaziranga can better be explored from height of 11 meter sitting at elephants back. Once the Elephant safari is over you may opt jeep safari activity in eastern range. After taking both safari return to resort and take your meal. Stay overnight at Kaziranga.

  • Days 04

    Kaziranga – Shillong

    After taking early morning breakfast drive to Shillong 280 kms 6 hrs drive. You may have your lunch en-route at Borapani Umiam Lake, a majestic placid lake with its surrounding sylvan hills. Do check in at your pre decided hotel. In the afternoon you can explore the local market. Stay Overnight at your hotel in Shillong.

  • Days 05

    Shillong – Cherrapunji – Waterfalls – Cave- Lake

    After having breakfast drive to Cherrapunjee which is 60 kms 1and 1/2 hr, the wettest place in the world. The beautiful waterfall-Noh-ka-likai is at Cherrapunjee you can also explore some of the caves in and around Cherrapunjee. Come back to Shillong, en-route visit Elephanta falls and Shillong peak for some breathtaking views. Stay overnight at your hotel in Shillong.

  • Days 06

    Shillong – Guwahati

    You can enjoy morning sightseeing in Shillong you can go Ward Lake and Hydari Park. Praise the beauty of Shillong, popularly called Scotland of the East. It is erstwhile capital of undivided Assam and present Northeast and home to the Khasi highland tribes. After having lunch drive back to Guwahati (100 kms). In the evening take a cruise on Mighty Brahmaputra River. Stay overnight at your hotel in Guwahati.

  • Days 07

    : Departure Onward Destination

    After breakfast complete check out formalities at the hotel and leave for Guwahati airport for your onward journey.
