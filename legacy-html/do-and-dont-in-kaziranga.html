Do’s and Don’ts in Kaziranga National Park

Kaziranga National Park located in Assam, India is a world-famous wildlife destination known for its greater one-horned rhinoceros, wild elephants, tigers, wild water buffaloes, and diverse birdlife. To protect this unique ecosystem and enjoy a safe, memorable safari experience, visitors should follow park regulations and responsible wildlife tourism practices. Whether you are planning a Jeep Safari an Elephant Safari where available, or a nature excursion around Kaziranga, following these essential guidelines helps preserve the park for future generations.

Do’s in Kaziranga National Park
  • Follow Safari Guide Instructions:
    Always listen to your authorized safari guide and driver. Follow their instructions throughout the safari, especially when observing wild animals or travelling through forest routes.
  • Carry Valid Identification and Booking Documents:
    Keep your valid identification and safari booking confirmation handy. Follow the entry requirements communicated by the authorized booking provider or park authorities.
  • Maintain Silence During the Safari:
    Speak softly and avoid unnecessary noise. A peaceful environment helps you observe wildlife naturally without disturbing animals or other visitors.
  • Maintain a Safe Distance from Wildlife:
    Observe rhinoceroses, elephants, deer, wild buffaloes, and other animals from a safe distance. Never attempt to approach, touch, feed, or provoke wildlife.
  • Wear Comfortable and Suitable Clothing:
    Choose comfortable clothing in natural or neutral colours. Carry a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, insect repellent, and weather-appropriate clothing. A raincoat may be useful during rainy weather.
  • Carry Essential Items:
    Bring drinking water, necessary personal medicines, a camera, binoculars, and a fully charged mobile phone. Carry only what you need and take all waste back with you.
  • Follow Designated Safari Routes:
    Stay on authorized safari routes and respect the instructions of forest officials. Entry into particular zones depends on official permissions, operating schedules, weather, and local conditions.
  • Respect Nature and Local Communities:
    Keep the surroundings clean, respect local customs, and support responsible local tourism services. Avoid activities that may harm wildlife or damage the natural habitat.
  • Use Cameras Responsibly:
    Photography is a wonderful way to remember your safari. Keep camera equipment secure, avoid flash if it could disturb wildlife, and follow your guide’s advice when photographing animals.
  • Check Safari Timings in Advance:
    Confirm your safari date, reporting time, entry point, identification requirements, and applicable rules before travelling. Safari availability and operating conditions may change according to official instructions.
Don’ts in Kaziranga National Park
  • Do Not Feed Wild Animals:
    Feeding wildlife can change natural behaviour, create dependency on humans, and expose animals to harmful food. Never offer food to any wild animal.
  • Do Not Litter:
    Never throw plastic bottles, food wrappers, cigarette butts, or other waste inside the park. Keep the forest clean and dispose of waste only at designated locations outside restricted wildlife areas.
  • Do Not Shout, Play Loud Music, or Use Horns:
    Loud sounds can frighten animals and disturb their natural behaviour. Keep your phone on silent mode and maintain a peaceful atmosphere during the safari.
  • Do Not Get Out of the Safari Vehicle Without Permission:
    Never leave your authorized safari vehicle unless your guide or the relevant authorities explicitly permit it at an authorized location. Wildlife can be unpredictable and dangerous.
  • Do Not Tease, Chase, or Provoke Animals:
    Never chase wildlife for photographs, throw objects at animals, or ask a driver to move dangerously close to them. Always prioritize the safety of visitors and animals.
  • Do Not Smoke or Light Fires in Restricted Areas:
    Avoid smoking and never light a fire inside the park. Fire can cause serious damage to grasslands, forests, and wildlife habitats. Follow all applicable fire-safety rules.
  • Do Not Carry Weapons or Prohibited Items:
    Do not bring firearms, explosives, or other prohibited items into the park. Follow official security checks and park entry regulations.
  • Do Not Pluck Plants or Damage the Environment:
    Do not pick flowers, break branches, damage trees, or remove natural objects from protected areas. Leave the habitat undisturbed.
  • Do Not Use Drones Without Authorization:
    Drone operations may be restricted or prohibited in protected wildlife areas. Never fly a drone unless you have obtained all permissions required by the relevant authorities.
  • Do Not Ignore Official Rules or Safety Warnings:
    Do not enter closed zones, cross barriers, or continue an activity against the instructions of forest officials. Park authorities may change access rules because of weather, wildlife movements, or conservation needs.
Important Safety Tips for Kaziranga Safari
  • Enter any area of the Park without appropriate permission is strictly prohibited.
  • Firearms, weapons, bows & arrows any sharp weapons, steels, matchbox and crackers are strictly prohibited inside the National Park.
  • Kindle fire or Campfire inside the Park is not allowed.
  • Don't blow horn inside the park.
  • Do not go on foot inside the Park except the permitted trails/ trek paths.
  • Don't tease wild animals
  • Don't feed wild animals/birds/ reptiles.
  • Don't disturb any herbs/ plants/ trees.
  • Collection of any kind of plants/ parts of plants is strictly prohibited.
  • Don't shout inside the park.
  • Don't attract or approach any wild animals.
  • Don't whistle inside the park.
  • Don't use any audio system inside the park.
  • Don't damage any signboards/ boundary pillars of the park.
  • Carrying fish, cooking inside the park is punishable offence
