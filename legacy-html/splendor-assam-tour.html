Splendor of Assam Tour

Duration

06 Night -07 Days

Location

Assam, India

Description

Assam is a state of natural wonderland and it has ample of picturesque beauty in its lap. Amalgamation of unique geographical condition, Assam throws broad picture of nature that is bestowed with greenery, wildlife, hills and array of wonderful things to be explore during trip. Going on Splendor of Assam Tour, you find an opportunity to visit few noted place of Assam which makes your holiday memorable. Here we suggest complete itinerary that customized by one of our expert tour guide keeping in mind budget perspective too.

Travel Days

  • Days 01

    Arrival at Guwahati

    Once you reach Guwahati airport or railway station you will be accompanied by our tour executive who will take you to the hotel booked for you in Guwahati. On reaching at hotel complete check-in formalities and freshen up yourself. Rest of the day at leisure; in evening you may go for city market for shopping. Stay overnight in the hotel.

  • Days 02

    Guwahati to Jorhat, Visit Tea Capital and The Thengal Masion

    Get up early in the morning and take breakfast at the hotel and leave for Jorhat which is 270 km. Jorhat is known as Tea capital of Assam. Upon arrival complete check-in formalities in the Thengal Mansion, a historic ancestral house of a Tea Planter amidst an Asssamese village, 15 kms from Jorhat. The Thangal Mansion is 72-year old that is enough to answer the queries on Assamese culture and tradition. Post lunch and rest, take a walk in a nearby village to see the simplicity of their lives. Stay overnight.

  • Days 03

    Jorhat – Siibsagar – Jorhat

    Start your day with delicious Assamese cuisines breakfast and drive to Sibsagar to visit remains of Ahom Kingdom who ruled Assam for 500 years before giving in to British. Here you have an opportunity to explore remains of the Ahom kingdom. Must not forget to visit Tai Ahom Museum and the Maidams (Mummy like structures) of Ahom kings Burial grounds at Charaideo. Return to Jorhat for Overnight stay.

  • Days 04

    Visit Toklai Research Station - kaziranga National Park

    Start your day with morning tea/coffee. Take breakfast on time and get ready to visit Toklai Research Station to know everything and anything about tea-plantation. Take lunch at Jorhat, post lunch we will leave for Kaziranga (90KM), an abode of of the rhino with one horn. Stay overnight at tourist lodge.

  • Days 05

    Explore Kaziranga National Park

    Day 5 of your tour is going to be more thrilling and adventurous. Get ready early morning 05:30 AM to unwind the central range of Kaziranga National Park on Elephant Safari ride. Once you finish elephant safari you can go for jeep safari ride in eastern range of the park. Return to resort to take lunch. Stay overnight.

  • Days 06

    Kaziranga – Guwahati

    After taking an early morning breakfast at the hotel complete check-out formalities it’s time to drive to Guwahati (220 kms 5 hrs of drive). In the evening take a cruise on Mighty Brahmaputra River. Stay overnight at your hotel in Guwahati.

  • Days 07

    Departure – Onward Destination

    After breakfast complete the check-out formalities at the hotel and leave for Guwahati airport of railway station. It’s time to leave for your sweet home with lots of memories.
